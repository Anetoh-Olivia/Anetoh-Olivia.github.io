About Me

I'm a data analyst who turns raw, often messy information into insights a business can actually use. My background is in Geology, and that training taught me to look for evidence before drawing conclusions, a habit that shaped how I approach every dataset I work with. I pay close attention to detail, think through a problem before I open a spreadsheet, and care more about a clear, honest answer than a flashy chart.

I work mainly in Power BI, Excel and SQL, and I've built everything from interactive dashboards to inventory tracking systems and statistical analyses using ANOVA. This portfolio brings together projects across retail, marketing, aviation and public data, each one built to answer a real question, not just display numbers.

Global Retail Sales Analysis: Investigating a Revenue Collapse

This project investigates why a global electronics retailer grew from $6.4M to $17.3M in revenue between 2016 and 2019, then fell below $1M by 2021. I cleaned six tables in Power Query, built a star schema in Power Pivot with DAX measures, and designed an interactive Excel dashboard to look for early warning signs in the data.

FitPulse Marketing Performance Analysis

I cleaned and normalised the data in PostgreSQL, wrote SQL funnel analysis, and built a 6-page Power BI dashboard covering awareness, attention, conversion, efficiency and budget decisions.

NYC Traffic Collision Analysis: High-Risk Times, Locations, Causes and Human Impact

This project analyses 238,000 NYPD-reported motor vehicle collisions to find out when, where and why accidents happen in New York City, and how deadly they are. I audited and cleaned the data in Power Query, built a date table, wrote DAX ranking measures that stay correct under any filter, and designed a 3-page Power BI dashboard covering time patterns, locations and causes, and severity.
